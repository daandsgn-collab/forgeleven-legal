Forgeleven Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Forgeleven ("we", "the app") shows how realistic football transfers currently look, using the Forgeleven Realism Score. This policy explains which data the app processes, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible: Forgeleven is a trade name of daandsgn, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) of Daan, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42231620, the Netherlands. Contact: daan.dsgn@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account, no name, no email address, no password.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising profile: ads are requested as non-personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are first asked for your ad privacy choice, which you can change at any time in Settings.
  • If you vote for the next Forgeleven Pick, we store a random voter number and your vote - nothing that identifies you.
  • Only if you turn on notifications do we store a push token and the clubs you follow.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Data stored only on your device

  • The club you last selected, so the app opens on that club.
  • Your favorite (followed) clubs and your notification setting.
  • A cached copy of the transfer data, the players you unlocked by watching an ad, and your Premium status as confirmed by the app store.
  • A random voter number (only once you open the Forgeleven Picks vote) and your ad privacy choice.

This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising profiles. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

How long we keep data

A push token and its followed clubs are kept while notifications are on. When you turn notifications off they are no longer used, and we delete tokens that are no longer valid. You can ask us to delete them at any time. A vote is kept while it is active; removing your vote in the app deletes it. Purchase records are kept by Apple/Google and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide and restore Premium and to meet legal obligations.

Ads

Free users can choose to watch an ad to unlock more recommendations. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and requested as non-personalized ads, so Google does not use your activity in Forgeleven to build an advertising profile. Google may still process the information listed above to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested; you can change it at any time via "Ad privacy choices" in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Premium (monthly subscription or lifetime) is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Purchases are verified through RevenueCat using an anonymous app user identifier, so your Premium status can be restored on a new installation.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are only requested after you follow a club or turn on transfer updates. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

People featured in Forgeleven (players)

Forgeleven reports on professional footballers. For each player we process their name, position, age, nationality, club and the transfer news published about them by media outlets, which we summarise in our own words with the name of the source. We do not process sensitive data such as health records beyond what is publicly reported as transfer news, and we never use player photos.

We do this based on our legitimate interest and the freedom of expression and information: informing football fans about publicly reported transfer news. Players under 18 are not shown in the app at all. Reports about them may be kept internally and are only published once the player is 18.

The full text of a news article is kept for at most 90 days to check our summaries; after that only the extracted facts (type of report, clubs, date, source) remain.

If you are a featured player (or their representative) and something is wrong, or you object to being included, email daan.dsgn@gmail.com. We will review and correct or remove the information as required by law, normally within 30 days.

Legal basis and your rights (EU/EEA and UK)

We process data to provide the features you use (performance of a contract), with your consent (notifications and, where required, ads), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and free through ads. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to data portability. Because the app has no accounts, the easiest way to delete your data is to turn off notifications and delete the app; you can also email daan.dsgn@gmail.com. You can file a complaint with your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

International transfers

Our server is located in Switzerland, which the EU recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. Google, Apple, Expo and RevenueCat may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Children

Forgeleven is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for digital services in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
